## Research supplement: sampler transfer and enlarged domain

This revision supplements the existing publication; it does not retract or silently replace the original theorem.

Added:

- `FOLLOWUP_RESEARCH_NOTE.md`: full statements and derivations for the even-selector domain through weight 300 with error weight at most 129, transfer through the inspected threshold and duplicate-rejection rule under IID source words, a fixed-context ideal-random-oracle corollary accounting for the finite counter, and a deterministic obstruction to unrestricted two-way aggregate-witness injectivity.
- A further finite-family corollary: with all generator parameters fixed, the probability over one ideal oracle that any 64-bit context tag fails the extended property or generation fails to finish is below 2^-98. This is not 98-bit HFHE security, a result for actual SHA-256, or cross-tag injectivity.

- `sparse_syndrome_checker.py`, `independent_recheck.py`, and their original baseline reports.

All work remains AI-assisted and not independently peer-reviewed. The algebraic aggregation result is not a demonstrated protocol exploit.
